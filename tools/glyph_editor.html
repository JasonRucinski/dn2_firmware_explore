Glyph Editor

For glyphs with frames, use the Glyph Animator.

Pick site/js/mods/glyphs.js to start.

Pick a tool and drag on the grid. The pencil's first pixel sets the colour of the stroke. Line, Rectangle and Ellipse draw from where you press to where you let go. Hold Shift for a square, a circle or a line in steps of 45 degrees. Erase, or the right mouse button, draws dark pixels. The previews show 1x, 2x and 3x in the light and the dark theme. You can also type or paste the 16 rows in the box: # is lit and . is dark.
